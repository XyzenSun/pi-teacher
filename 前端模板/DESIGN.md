## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a mindful, scholarly atmosphere tailored for deep cognitive work, synthesis, and focused learning. At the intersection of deliberate structure (Linear), tactile spatial simplicity (Notion), and refined digital stationery (Apple Notes), the system eliminates visual friction so the user’s mind remains immersed in analysis, retention, and insight.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Contemplative & Composed:** Quiet surfaces and subdued contrast calm the visual field, preventing cognitive fatigue during prolonged study sessions.
- **Precise & Systematic:** Crisp alignments, purposeful borders, and modular information blocks engender trust in automated AI summaries and knowledge capture.
- **Tactile & Scholarly:** Evoking high-grade archival paper and ink, the aesthetic dignifies the user's intellectual output without decorative indulgence.

### Design Movement
The style is a synthesis of **Warm Architectural Minimalism** and **Editorial Workspace Design**. Rather than stark industrial sterility or playful dopamine-driven software, it prioritizes generous breathing room, quiet functional hierarchy, hairline structural framing, and soft warm-light paper tonalities.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around the optical comfort of long-form reading, note retrieval, and AI interaction. It exchanges harsh synthetic values for organic, ink-and-paper tonalities.

### Color Tiers & Roles
- **Primary (`#1E252B` - Deep Contemplative Slate):** The color of carbon ink. Used for authoritative typography, primary navigation anchors, and dominant interactive buttons.
- **Secondary (`#5B7C6E` - Soothing Sage):** A calibrated botanical green providing restorative focus. Applied to active knowledge node highlights, synthesis status indicators, study streaks, and primary AI generative accents.
- **Tertiary (`#8C7355` - Warm Umber / Kraft):** Derived from binding bookcloth and archival card stock. Employed sparingly for secondary tags, flashcard intervals, and annotation markers.
- **Neutral Surface Palette:**
  - **Base Canvas (`#F9F8F6` - Warm Alabaster):** Replaces pure digital blue-white with a warm, unbleached linen tone, reducing ocular stress.
  - **Elevated Canvas / Sheet (`#FFFFFF` - Crisp White):** Reserved strictly for note sheets, active reading panes, and workspace editor panels to establish clear spatial elevation.
  - **Subtle Surface (`#F2EFEB` - Soft Sand):** Used for sidebar rails, inactive cards, and docked tool trays.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E5E2DC` / `#D4CFC7`):** Hairline structure with warm undertones that avoids harsh slate or stark gray edges.
- **Text Hierarchies:**
  - `Text-Primary`: `#1A1D20` (High-contrast slate ink)
  - `Text-Secondary`: `#55595F` (Muted pencil lead)
  - `Text-Tertiary`: `#8A8E94` (Quiet captions and metadata)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes the structural utility of modern technical tools with the contemplative cadence of academic publishing.

### Dual-Type Strategy
1. **Primary Interface Sans (`Inter`):** Drives UI controls, navigation trees, metadata, prompts, and analytical metrics. Crisp, neutral, and unobtrusive.
2. **Editorial Serif (`Newsreader`):** Applied selectively to long-form reading panes, essay synthesis, note document titles, and reflective quotes. This invites slower, more attentive comprehension.
3. **CJK Fallback Integration:** In bilingual or East Asian study contexts, pair `Inter` seamlessly with `PingFang SC` / `Noto Sans SC` at matching optical weights, and `Newsreader` with `Songti SC` / `Noto Serif SC` for long-form reading passages.

### Formatting & Readability Rules
- Maintain reading text column widths between `56ch` and `68ch` for optimal scanning.
- Utilize the `body-reading` style with `Newsreader` for AI synthesis outputs and book excerpts, ensuring line heights remain generous (`1.6`–`1.7x`) to prevent eye fatigue.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture is grounded in an authentic workstation mental model: a quiet multi-column pane interface that transitions gracefully into a distraction-free single-sheet layout.

### Rhythm & Grid Architecture
- **Base Grid:** Strict 8px baseline rhythm with 4px sub-increments for micro-alignments, tags, and inline badges.
- **Three-Pane Architecture (Desktop):**
  - **Navigation & Library Index (`gutter-sidebar` / 256px):** Collapsible file tree, knowledge base clusters, and search.
  - **Active Canvas / Document (Flexible Center):** Pinned to an ergonomic reading max-width of `44rem` (704px) when in single-editor mode, stretching fluidly up to `84rem` when side-by-side card references are open.
  - **AI Context & Inspector (`gutter-aside` / 352px):** Docked right panel for citations, prompt dialogue, contextual flashcards, and summary graphs.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Reflow
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** Simultaneous 3-column workstation state with persistent sidebars and sheet view.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** Inspector slides into an overlay drawer; left sidebar collapses into an icon-rail with an expandable flyout. Focus stays centered on the central document.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Complete reflow to a bottom-tabbed single-sheet flow. Toolbars dock cleanly above the soft keyboard with 16px safe side margins.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is articulated through physical surface tiers and gentle ambient illumination, avoiding harsh digital drop-shadows or high-contrast borders.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Base Layer (Warm Alabaster `#F9F8F6`):** The structural desk surface. Lowest tier.
2. **Sub-Panels (Soft Sand `#F2EFEB`):** Navigational trees, docked toolbars, and inactive panels sitting directly atop the desk with flush hairline separators (`#E5E2DC`).
3. **Document Canvas (Crisp White `#FFFFFF`):** Floats subtly above the base surface, framed with a 1px border of `#E5E2DC`.
4. **Floating Overlays & Menus:** Omnisearch dialogs, AI command palettes, and floating contextual toolbars sit at the highest tier.

### Ambient Shadow Characteristics
Shadows simulate soft, diffused daylight from an overhead lamp:
- **Sheet Elevation (Resting):** `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(30, 37, 43, 0.03), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(30, 37, 43, 0.02)`.
- **Active Card / Dragged Block:** `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(30, 37, 43, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(30, 37, 43, 0.03)`.
- **Command Palette / Modal:** `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(30, 37, 43, 0.08), 0 0 0 1px rgba(30, 37, 43, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The geometry reflects tailored, high-end product stationery. Surfaces maintain structural poise with deliberate, restrained radius tokens between 8px and 12px.

### Corner Curvature Rules
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons, Segmented Tabs):** `0.5rem` (8px) for a focused, calibrated tactile footprint.
- **Containers & Cards (Study Cards, Source Callouts, AI Drawers):** `0.625rem` (10px) to `0.75rem` (12px) to softly demarcate distinct concepts without turning playful or bulbous.
- **Modals & Flyouts:** `0.875rem` (14px) outer shell with concentric internal corner nestings of `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Inline Badges & Meta Pills:** `0.375rem` (6px) or fully rounded pill (`9999px`) exclusively for lightweight numerical counters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Deep Slate (`#1E252B`) background with pure white typography, 8px border-radius, subtle interior light border highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`). Hover triggers a transition to `#2E373F`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with 1px border (`#E5E2DC`) and slate text. Hover yields a faint warm wash (`#F2EFEB`).
- **AI Action:** Sage accent treatment (`#5B7C6E` surface or `rgba(91, 124, 110, 0.1)` tint with `#3E584D` text) and an icon prefix.

### Chips & Meta Tags
- **Knowledge Chips:** Subtle warm paper background (`#F2EFEB`), 1px hairline border (`#E5E2DC`), 6px radius, `label-md` Inter typography.
- **Concept / Topic Tags:** Tinted with Sage (`#5B7C6E` at 12% opacity) or Umber (`#8C7355` at 12% opacity) to categorize subjects without cognitive noise.

### Cards & Knowledge Blocks
- **Document Card:** Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) surface, 1px `#E5E2DC` boundary, 10px radius, resting shadow. On hover, the border shifts slightly to `#D4CFC7` with a 2px vertical lift.
- **AI Synthesis / Insight Card:** Framed with a 1px border of `#5B7C6E` at 30% opacity, a top accent stroke of Sage (`#5B7C6E`), and a 0.5rem inner padding offset.

### Input Fields & Search (Command Bar)
- **Editor & Field Surfaces:** Clean `#FFFFFF` or `#F9F8F6` inset. Border is 1px `#E5E2DC`.
- **Focus State:** Crisp 1.5px border of `#5B7C6E` (Sage) accompanied by a soft halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(91, 124, 110, 0.12)`. No harsh blue focus outlines.
- **Omnisearch (Command Palette):** Centered modal with a large `headline-md` prompt line, minimal hairline dividers, and keyboard shortcut icons enclosed in `#F2EFEB` capsules.

### Checkboxes, Radios, & Flashcard Controls
- **Checkboxes:** 18px rounded squares (4px radius) with hairline borders. Checked state fills with `#1E252B` (or `#5B7C6E` for completed study goals) displaying a micro white tick.
- **Spaced Repetition Rating Buttons:** A 4-tier horizontal button dock (Again, Hard, Good, Easy) utilizing muted neutral states that reveal tinted accents only on active focus.

### Lists & Navigation Trees
- **Tree Node Rows:** Clean 32px height, 6px hover radius, faint `#F2EFEB` background on hover. Selected document row features a 2px vertical left indicator bar in `#1E252B` with semi-bold text.
